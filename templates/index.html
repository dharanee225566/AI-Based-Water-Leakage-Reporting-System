HOW AQUAWATCH AI OPERATES

From Citizen Report to Field Repair in Minutes

An automated end-to-end municipal pipeline designed to eliminate delays, prevent repeat dispatches, and accelerate hydraulic repairs.

Step 1

1. Citizen Reports Leak

Citizens upload leak photos, drop GPS pin, and describe the hazard on desktop or smartphone without app downloads.

Step 2

2. AI Classification & Triage

Natural language model categorizes leak type, flags duplicates using Haversine distance, and computes priority score.

Step 3

3. Field Crew Dispatch

Maintenance staff receives concise AI Field Summaries, navigational coordinates, and work orders directly on mobile dashboards.

Step 4

4. Verified Resolution

Crew completes repair, records pressure metrics, closes ticket, and notifies citizen with transparent audit timeline.

INTELLIGENT WATER DEFENSE

Engineered to Solve Real Municipal Headaches

Traditional reporting hotlines suffer from flood of duplicate calls, misclassified tickets, and delayed dispatches. AquaWatch AI solves this with four specialized machine learning capabilities:

Sub-Second NLP Complaint Classification

Categorizes into pipe burst, tap leak, roadway flooding, meter damage, or sewage contamination automatically.

Spatial & Textual Duplicate Detection

Calculates Haversine distance and TF-IDF cosine similarity to merge redundant citizen tickets and avoid double dispatch.

Dynamic Priority & Water Loss Estimation

Ranks urgency from High to Low and estimates Liters/Hour water loss to prioritize critical transmission line bursts first.

AI Field Crew Dispatch Summaries

Synthesizes clean 2-sentence actionable instructions with hazard advisories for field technicians on mobile devices.

Identified Urban Hotspot Streets

AI analyzes historical complaints to detect aging conduit corridors requiring proactive replacement.
